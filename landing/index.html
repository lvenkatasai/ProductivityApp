The fine print.

Does it need the internet?

No. Productivity OS is a local desktop app. It installs once and runs fully offline. Nothing about you leaves your machine.

Where does my data live?

In one SQLite file on your disk. Copy it, back it up, take it to a new PC. There is no server and no account to hold it hostage.

Is a streak broken by one miss?

No. Scores are append-only: a weak day records what happened, and the streak survives it. Only an unstarted day breaks discipline.

What are quests made of?

Counts. Pages, kilometres, sets, minutes. Core weight 3x, standard 2x, bonus points above. Numeric quests close when the count is met; a day closes when you say so.

Which Windows versions?

Windows 10 (1809 or newer) and Windows 11, 64-bit. A native installer and an exe you can place anywhere; no driver tricks, no admin rights for the download.
